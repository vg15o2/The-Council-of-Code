# 🏛️ The Council of Code

> “All who wander are not lost… some are just debugging.” – *Gandalf the Grey,glancing at a segfault*

### A Mythic Crossover

This is **what happens when Rivendell meets Albuquerque**, when **Elves write algorithms**, and **Saul Goodman reverse-engineers recursion**.  
It’s *The Lord of the Rings* meets *Breaking Bad*, with a side of *Better Call Saul* — a tale of **wizards, data structures, and clever loopholes**.

Welcome to **The Council of Code**, where the spirit of Middle-earth blends with the grit of the desert… and together, they **compile**.

### ⚔️ The Order of Algorithms

- **Samwise the Sorter** (Bubble Sort) – Loyal, slow, but never gave up.
- **Boromir of Brute Force** (Selection Sort) – Noble, but fell to time.
- **Frodo the Inserter** (Insertion Sort) – Carried the burden one element at a time.
- **Aragorn of Pivots** (Quick Sort) – Led the charge, split the lands, sorted the kingdoms.
- **Gimli of the Heap** (Heap Sort) – Unearthed power, toppled it, rebuilt again.
- **Gandalf the Recursive** (Merge Sort) – Divided to conquer, returned as O(n log n).
- **Legolas the Counter** (Counting Sort) – Saw the range, struck with elegance.
- **The Eagles of Radix** – Flew over comparisons, sorting digits from the sky.
- **Éowyn of Greedy** – Took what was best in the moment… and survived.
- **Faramir of Backtracking** – Tried all paths, with nobility and regret.
- **Bilbo of Recursion** – There and back again, endlessly.
- **Mike Ehrmantraut** – Debugged without words. Solved without mercy.
